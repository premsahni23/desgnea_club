The details that matter before choosing a position.

Can I apply for President or Vice President?
No. These two roles are part of the leadership structure but are not open in this registration.
Can I apply for more than one position?
Choose one position that best fits your interests when you apply. The team can discuss other suitable roles with you during the selection process.
Do I need professional experience?
No. Tell us what you enjoy doing, what you have tried so far, and what you would like to learn.
Can students from every ASD year apply?
Yes. Students from every ASD year can apply for the open team positions.
Does joining guarantee an internship or certificate?
No. Joining the club does not guarantee an internship or certificate. Any opportunities will be communicated separately.
Can non-team members join club activities?
Yes. ASD students can take part in club activities that are announced as open to everyone.
